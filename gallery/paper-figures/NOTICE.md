# Research paper figure attribution

The three `final.png` files in this directory were supplied by the project maintainer as **Paper Fig-generated results**, not as the original paper figures. `preview.webp` is a lossless, full-resolution re-encoding of each supplied PNG. These examples show figure reconstruction, not the reproduction of a paper's experiments or a controlled model benchmark.

The reference cases were discovered through [PaperGallery](https://github.com/LongHZ140516/PaperGallery), whose [Pipeline / FlowChart collection](https://longhz140516.github.io/PaperGallery/) credits the underlying papers.

| Case | Original research | Reference entry |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `compact3d` | [CompGS: Smaller and Faster Gaussian Splatting with Vector Quantization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18159) | [PaperGallery: Compact3D](https://longhz140516.github.io/PaperGallery/images/pipeline-Navaneet_Compact3D_2024/) |
